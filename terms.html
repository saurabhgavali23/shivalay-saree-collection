3. Handloom & Color Disclaimers

Please note the following intrinsic qualities of handwoven Indian textiles:

  • Artisanal Weaving Marks: Genuine handloom sarees are crafted on traditional wooden pit looms or handlooms. Minor yarn slubs, micro-irregularities in the weft, tiny knots, or extra zari thread ends on the reverse side are natural hallmarks of human handcraftsmanship, not manufacturing defects.
  • Digital Screen Shade Variations: We strive to capture our saree photography and drape videos in natural and studio lighting. However, due to variations in mobile device screens, monitor calibrations, brightness settings, and fabric luster (particularly pure silk and metallic zari), slight shade differences (5%–10%) between the online visual and physical saree may occur.
  • Live WhatsApp Video Verification: To eliminate all ambiguity, we enthusiastically offer a Live WhatsApp Video Call prior to dispatch upon customer request, allowing you to examine the drape, pallu border, and true daylight color before shipping.
🔍
Subjective Perceptions: Differences in perceived color tone, stiffness of unwashed pure silk, or personal draping style preferences do not qualify as defects or grounds for returns.

4. Dimensions & Blouse Pieces

Unless explicitly described otherwise on the saree's detail page:

  • Total Running Length: Standard sarees feature an overall running length of approximately 6.3 meters (6.2m to 6.3m).
  • Saree Body: Approximately 5.5 meters of drape fabric, including the pleated body, borders, and woven pallu.
  • Unstitched Blouse Piece: Approximately 0.8 meters of attached running fabric, meant to be cut and tailored to your custom measurements.
  • Do Not Detach Before Inspection: If you receive your parcel, inspect the saree with the unstitched blouse piece completely intact. Cutting or detaching the blouse piece voids any claim eligibility.

8. Strict Return & Mandatory Unboxing Video Policy

Due to the delicate nature of pure silk, zari embroidery, and bespoke handcrafted textiles, we adhere to a strictly defined return policy:

🚨
No Exchange Policy: We do not offer exchanges, replacements, or returns for change-of-mind, occasion postponement, or subjective color preferences. Please inspect all photos, videos, and specifications before booking.

Conditions for Return (Damaged Items Only)

Returns are accepted strictly and exclusively if a genuine physical defect or transit damage is discovered upon arrival:

  1. Mandatory Continuous Uncut 360° Unboxing Video: A single, uncut, unedited, full unboxing video is strictly mandatory. The video must clearly capture:
    • The sealed outer parcel and clear visibility of our shipping label with tracking number.
    • The continuous cutting of the outer courier tamper-evident seal and opening.
    • The complete unfolding of the saree and immediate highlighting of the claimed physical damage.
    Claims submitted without an uncut, continuous unboxing video will be summarily declined.
  2. Timely Notification: Any damage claim must be reported to our WhatsApp support lines within 24 hours of parcel delivery marked by the courier system.
  3. Customer Self-Ship Return: Once the damage claim is approved by our team, the customer must courier / self-ship the saree back to our designated return address using a traceable courier service.
  4. Condition of Returned Item: The saree must be in its original, unworn, unwashed state, in original folding, with all original tags attached and the unstitched blouse piece completely intact.
  5. Refund Processing: Upon safe receipt and physical inspection of the returned package at our warehouse, a full refund of the purchase amount will be credited to the customer via UPI within 2 business days.
